My Ruby, my Jaan,

Happy birthday to the most caring, most lovable, most beautiful soul I have ever known. Today the world celebrates you, but I have been celebrating you every single day since you became mine.

You love people the way candles give light — quietly, completely, without ever asking for anything back. I have watched you take care of everyone around you, always the first to help, always the one who shows up. And somehow, in the middle of taking care of the whole world, you still find room to take care of me too. I don't think you even realise how rare that is. I notice it every day, and I love you more for it every day.

I love you more than my own life, Ruby. Not as a line, not as something poetic to say — I mean it the way the sky means the stars. You are stitched into every plan I make for my future. When I picture being happy, you are already standing in that picture.

So today, I don't just want to wish you happy birthday. I want you to feel like the queen of this entire world — because to me, that's exactly what you are. Every star in tonight's sky is just my small attempt at building you a crown.

— Your Gaurav, forever
